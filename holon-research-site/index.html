Holon Research

Holon Research provides the infrastructure for private, sovereign intelligence. A necessity now that AI agents are shaping how individuals and companies think, decide, and act.

We are working towards a world of specialized continual learning models running on your own infrastructure, training on what makes you or your company distinct.

Four things make this possible:

Pre-built hardware: prebuilt computers with GPUs, thermally-tested, designed to run 24/7 inference.

Model runtime: A stable model runtime to run local models with just a few clicks. Equipped with cloud grade caching mechanism, concurrency, and optimizations.

Harness: a local-first harness designed to work with our model runtime for efficient context management and throughput.

Improvement loop: owning the model, data, and harness means you can specialize your models on your tasks, getting better as you use it. We are working on an on-device training mechanism to allow for an experience that adapts to your world.
